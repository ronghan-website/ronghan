About

I am a mathematician working in the field of analysis and partial differential equations. My research focuses on the theoretical and computational aspects of PDEs, with applications to mathematical physics and scientific computing.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from [University Name] in [Year]. Prior to my current position, I held postdoctoral positions at [Institution 1] and [Institution 2].

Research interests: Functional analysis, partial differential equations, numerical methods, mathematical modeling, scientific computing.

Research

My research focuses on the analysis and numerical solution of partial differential equations arising in physics and engineering. Current areas of interest include:

  • Functional Analysis: Study of infinite-dimensional vector spaces, operator theory, and spectral analysis.
  • Partial Differential Equations: Analysis of elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic equations with various boundary conditions.
  • Numerical Methods: Development and analysis of finite element methods, finite difference schemes, and iterative solvers.
  • Mathematical Modeling: Applications of PDEs to real-world phenomena in physics, biology, and engineering.
  • Computational Mathematics: High-performance computing for large-scale PDE problems.
