About

I am a second-year PhD student in Mathematics at the Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu-Paris Rive Gauche (IMJ-PRG) and the Département de Mathématiques et Applications (DMA), under the supervision of Julien Marché and Gregor Masbaum. My research interests focus on quantum topology.

Research interests: Quantum topology, topological quantum field theory, knot invariants.

Research

My research focuses on quantum topology and related areas in mathematical physics.

  • Quantum Topology: Study of quantum invariants of knots and links, skein algebras, and skein modules.
  • Topological Quantum Field Theory: Applications of TQFT to the study of three-dimensional manifolds, including semiclassical limits.
